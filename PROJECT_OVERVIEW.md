# 🚀 UstaadJi: AI Service Orchestrator

**UstaadJi** is an AI-powered digital orchestrator designed to formalize and automate the informal service economy (e.g., plumbers, electricians, AC repair) in Pakistan. 

By simply sending a natural language message describing their problem, users interact with a 5-agent AI pipeline that automatically extracts their intent, discovers the nearest real-world service providers using geocoding, ranks the best options based on reviews and distance, and estimates fair market pricing. 

The result is a seamless, conversational, and highly accurate mobile booking experience that bridges the gap between everyday consumers and reliable local tradespeople.
